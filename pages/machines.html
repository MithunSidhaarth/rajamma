Core capabilities

Eight machining disciplines. One standard of accuracy.

Horizontal Boring

True, in-line bores across large fabricated bodies and machine bases, on both CNC and conventional horizontal boring machines.

CNC Boring

Programmable multi-face boring and milling on our Droop & Rein CNC horizontal boring machine for repeatable tolerance across batches.

high repeatability

Plano Milling

Wide-bed flat and profile milling, guideway machining and surface finishing on large fabricated plates and beds.

large-bed capability

Radial Drilling

Precision drilling, tapping, reaming, countersinking and hole enlargement across large, awkward-to-fixture parts.

3 radial drills

Precision & Surface Milling

Fine-tolerance face, profile and surface milling for critical mating surfaces and structural machining.

fine finish

Alignment & Line Boring

Multi-datum alignment and line boring across large structures, machined true in as few setups as possible.

single-datum setups

Machine Base & Structure Machining

Structural bases, beds and fabricated weldments machined true for OEM machine builders and industrial plants.

up to 15T

Emergency & Repair Machining

priority slotting
