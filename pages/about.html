About Rajamma Engineering

Thirty years of holding heavy work to tolerance.

A machining-only shop in Ambattur Industrial Estate, Chennai — built for the components that need to be right the first time.

Our story

Started in 1993, on one conviction.

Rajamma Engineering Pvt. Ltd. was founded in 1993 in Ambattur Industrial Estate on a simple premise: a shop that only machines — never fabricates — will always machine better than one that does both. Three decades later, that focus is still the whole business.

We take fabricated structures our customers have already built — machine bases, mill housings, turbine frames, kiln supports — and bring them to the tolerance the drawing calls for. Nothing about our process is optimised for welding convenience. Every fixture, every setup, every operator hour is optimised for accuracy on heavy work.

Today that means components up to 15 tons, machined on horizontal boring, CNC boring, plano milling and radial drilling equipment, with emergency job support when a customer's downtime can't wait for a normal queue.
