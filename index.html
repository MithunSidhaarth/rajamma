Who we are

A machining partner, not a fabricator.

Founded in 1993 in Ambattur Industrial Estate, Rajamma Engineering does one thing: precision machining of heavy, customer-supplied fabricated structures. We don't fabricate — three decades of machining only means every hour of investment has gone into boring, milling and drilling accuracy on components other shops turn away.

  • Machining only — no fabrication conflicts of interest
  • Components up to 15 tons on a single setup
  • Emergency job support when a breakdown can't wait

How we work

From drawing to dispatch.

01

Drawing & RFQ review

Share your GA drawing and tolerance requirements. Our engineers review fixturing and machining feasibility before quoting.

Typical turnaround: 24–48 hours
02

Fixture & process planning

Heavy components are planned for single-setup machining wherever possible, to hold datum accuracy across all features.

03

Machining

Horizontal boring, CNC boring, plano milling or radial drilling — executed to the drawing's stated GD&T.

04

Inspection

Every critical dimension is measured and recorded against the drawing before sign-off.

05

Dispatch

Components are packed and dispatched with inspection reports, ready to install.
